# Gameplay balance and simulation notes

This document distinguishes the fixed Campaign simulation from the open-mode
rules. The committed `docs/balance-report.txt` is regenerated by:

```sh
./build/test_game --balance 30
```

Open modes have a separate reproducible expert-bot report:

```sh
./build/test_game --open-balance 10 0x1a2b3c4d
```

See [`open-mode-balance-report.md`](open-mode-balance-report.md) for its exact
seed policy, sample size, methodology, and results. Do not mix these open-mode
results with the Campaign-only report below.

That command currently measures **Campaign only**: 30 scripted bot runs per
character and skill level. It is useful as a repeatable regression baseline,
but bots are not human players and the report does not measure Endless or Boss
Rush completion rates. Do not reuse historical open-mode percentages without
rerunning the matching simulation against this source revision.

## Campaign preservation baseline

The current Campaign bot report was compared with the original supplied
`init-wars(2).tar.gz` source build. The bot metrics and hit-source rows match
exactly after removing the final `speed:` line, which is machine- and
run-dependent. This is the repeatable evidence that the open-mode work did not
alter Campaign balance output.

Campaign keeps its ten authored waves, story scenes, and original maintenance
breaks at waves 4, 7, and 10. Cron and the OOM killer are available to Endless
Mode but are intentionally not inserted into the Campaign wave definitions.

## Endless Mode

Endless waves use a deterministic threat budget. The initial budget is
`12 + 3 * wave`, capped at 100. The generated queue is bounded by `MAX_QUEUE`.
The available enemy pool expands in stages:

| Wave reached | Newly available pool size | Newly unlocked enemies |
|---:|---:|---|
| 1 | 1 | journald |
| 2 | 2 | logind |
| 3 | 3 | networkd |
| 4 | 4 | timer |
| 5 | 5 | udevd |
| 6 | 7 | resolved, dependency.target |
| 7 | 9 | cron, OOM killer |

`Restart=always` starts appearing at wave 4. Its chance grows by 2.5
percentage points per wave, starting at 4%, and is capped at 38%.

Open-mode regular enemy health is scaled by `1 + 0.035 * (wave - 1)`, capped at
1.75 times base health. Enemy movement, attack cadence, and projectiles scale
with the Endless tempo multiplier `1 + 0.025 * (wave - 1)`, capped at 1.65.
Every tenth wave is a systemd boss encounter. The first boss uses base health;
each later tenth-wave tier adds 35% of base boss health. The mode continues
after boss waves. Entering every seventh wave restores 1 HP when the player is
below maximum HP.

## Boss Rush

Boss Rush starts with six upgrade selections before round one, then presents
eight consecutive systemd boss encounters. Boss health is base health plus 35%
of base health for each later round (round 8 is 3.45 times base health).
Enemy movement, attack cadence, and projectile speed increase by 4% per round,
up to 1.50 times normal tempo. Each cleared round before the final encounter
offers another upgrade and restores up to 3 HP. The final round ends the run;
there is no between-round selection after round eight.

## Open-mode upgrade safeguards

These safeguards apply only to Endless Mode and Boss Rush:

- Special-recharge upgrades cap at three stacks.
- HP, fire-rate, damage, and movement upgrades cap at five stacks each.
- Upgrade choices already at their cap are removed from the available pool.
- If every upgrade is capped, the selection becomes HP repair patches.
- BusyBox's emergency special cooldown is multiplied by 1.5 (50% longer).

The Campaign upgrade path and BusyBox cooldown are not modified by these
open-mode safeguards.

## Current open-mode bot snapshot

A fresh 10-run-per-character expert-bot study is recorded in
[`open-mode-balance-report.md`](open-mode-balance-report.md). In that sample,
Endless mean wave ranged from 8.70 (OpenRC) to 15.40 (runit); no character
completed Boss Rush, and mean bosses cleared ranged from 0.90 to 2.10. Those
figures are specific to the documented deterministic seed set and bot profile.
An earlier quoted 10-run table differs and could not be reproduced from
its original run logs or seed policy, so it remains a comparison note rather
than a verified baseline.

## Current Campaign bot snapshot

The exact generated output is in [`balance-report.txt`](balance-report.txt).
The current report shows the boss as the principal difficulty spike. Bot win
rates vary substantially by skill and character; that is expected evidence for
further tuning, not a substitute for a human playtest.
